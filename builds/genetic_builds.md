# Recommended Genetic Builds (Mid-rate)

## 1. Cart Cannon Glass Cannon
- **Goal:** Highest single-target / AoE physical damage
- **Stats:** DEX 120+, INT 80–100, LUK 60+, rest AGI/VIT
- **Weapon:** High base ATK + Cannon-friendly cards
- **Homunculus:** Eleanor or Sera
- **AI:** Aggressive + UseAttackSkill on

## 2. Acid Demonstration Caster
- **Goal:** Magic acid damage + utility
- **Stats:** INT / DEX / LUK focus
- **Key:** High Prepare Potion success rate
- **Homunculus:** Sera for party support

## 3. Homunculus Carry
- **Goal:** Homunculus does most of the killing
- **Owner:** Support / tank / utility
- **Homunculus S:** Bayeri, Eleanor, or Dieter depending on map
- **AI:** Full aggressive + good tactics file

## Card Priority (general)
1. Size card matching most monsters you farm
2. Race card
3. Element card
4. Flat ATK / MATK or special Genetic cards
5. Defensive cards on armor/garment for survivability
