# Philosopher’s Stone & Related Items

These items are intentionally rare and expensive.

| Item ID (suggested) | Name                     | Purpose |
|---------------------|--------------------------|---------|
| 19620               | Stone of the Sage        | High-tier catalyst, required for Philosopher’s Stone attempt |
| 19621               | Philosopher’s Stone      | Ultimate alchemy catalyst (very low success rate) |
| 19622               | Sage Fragment            | Intermediate currency toward the stones |

## Design Notes
- Stone of the Sage: achievable with dedication (60% success in the fusion NPC).
- Philosopher’s Stone: deliberately hard (15% base success, huge material cost).
- Both should be account-bound or character-bound on most servers.
- Can be used later as catalysts for exclusive Genetic / Homunculus gear upgrades.

Adjust the success percentages and material counts in `npc/philosopher_stone_fusion.txt` to match your server’s economy.
